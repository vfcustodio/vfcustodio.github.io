## Design Context

### Users
Recruiters, hiring managers, founders, and marketing leaders evaluating Vinicius for growth and performance marketing roles. They need to understand his scope quickly, verify experience, and contact him without friction.

### Brand Personality
Analytical, confident, human. The interface should feel credible, sharp, and quietly ambitious.

### Aesthetic Direction
Editorial portfolio with an operations-minded edge: warm paper, deep ink, teal signal color, crisp rules, and asymmetric two-column composition. Light mode only. Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, neon gradients, and over-rounded card grids.

### Design Principles
- Make the career narrative scannable before making it decorative.
- Use typography and rules as the main visual system.
- Surface proof of range without overwhelming the reader.
- Keep contact and the PDF one click away.
- Preserve a calm, high-contrast reading experience on mobile.
